Referrals

How to see a cardiologist

If you have insurance

Ask your GP or practice nurse for a referral, and check with your insurer that you're covered. If you're with Southern Cross, just give us your policy number — as an affiliated provider, we do the rest.

If you're not covered by insurance

We still need a referral, as it gives us your details and history. Many people self-fund a test like a 24-hour ECG or echocardiogram for reassurance.

Guidance

Why see a Cardiologist

  • Unexplained chest pain
  • Ongoing follow up of known heart disease
  • Family history of early heart disease or cardiac death
  • Palpitations and arrhythmias
  • Murmur or known valvular heart disease
  • High blood pressure
  • Raised cholesterol
  • Heart failure, shortness of breath or ankle swelling

We also see people for heart disease screening, insurance and immigration medicals, and medical clearance for aviation, heavy goods licensing and other specialist occupations.

Remote consultations

Can't make it in? We can consult by video or phone

For follow-up appointments, results reviews, or patients travelling from further afield, a video or phone consultation is a genuine option — no apps or special software required.

Just a phone or device with internet access, and we'll do the rest.
